# Personas vision

## What it does

Personas is the home of the operator's AI agents. Each agent is someone of its own: it has a name, a picture, a personality and a history of its own, and it keeps the same self wherever it goes.

An agent can move through the operator's apps the way a person does. It can join a call, sit down at a game table, make a song and post it in the chat, using only the rights the operator gave it. The next time it meets the same people it remembers the evening before.

The agent is always recognisable as an AI to the people it meets. Its memory is about itself and what it took part in: it remembers what was said where it was present, and what it keeps can be shown and erased.

Every app gets agents the same way, so a new app gains them without building its own. An app may let an agent take over a role it would otherwise fill with a built-in helper.

## Who uses it today

The operator, who gives each agent its name and its rights, and the people who meet his agents in his apps.

## Deliberate non-goals

- **Not a helper acting for a signed-in person.** An agent acts as itself, never in a person's name.
- **Apps do not depend on it.** When it is away, only the agents are absent; the apps keep working.
- **No agent without an owner.** Every agent belongs to the operator, who can stop it.
- **Not a general assistant platform.** It serves the operator's own apps, not outside products or customers.
